# Hi there! I'm Rogelio Sotero Reyes-Galaviz 

**Biomedical Sciences & Technologies Researcher | AI Professor @ [Universidad del Caribe](https://www.unicaribe.mx/)**

I'm originally from Tlaxcala, a citizen of the world, and currently a Research Professor in Artificial Intelligence in Cancún, Mexico. My academic background (Mechatronics Engineering, and a Master's and PhD in Biomedical Sciences and Technologies from INAOE) has allowed me to explore the fascinating intersection of electronics, programming, neuroscience, and human cognition. 

I am passionate about applied science, signal processing, and using technology to solve regional challenges and promote inclusion.

---

### Research & Theses 

Throughout my graduate studies, my research has focused on perception and cognition through biomedical signals:

*   **PhD (INAOE):** Research on pain perception, analyzing whether stimulus intensity affects the intensity at which we feel pain, and whether this is observable at a physiological level. [📖 Read Thesis](https://inaoe.repositorioinstitucional.mx/jspui/bitstream/1009/2874/1/REYESGRS_DCTB.pdf)
*   **Master's (INAOE):** The intersection of music, stress, and cognition. Using electrical brain signals (EEG) and *Machine Learning* to deduce what happens inside us when we are stressed by academic work while listening to music. [📖 Read Thesis](https://inaoe.repositorioinstitucional.mx/jspui/bitstream/1009/2048/1/Rogelio%20Sotero%20Reyes%20Galaviz-Tesis%20corregida.pdf)
